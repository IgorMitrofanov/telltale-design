03 — The venue

Brewery on the Leie

Brouwerij Sint-Pieters
Kuiperskaai 12, 9000 Gent

By train

Ghent-Sint-Pieters station is 12 minutes on foot, or tram 1 to Zuid (4 stops). Last trains to Brussels and Bruges leave after 00:30 during the festival.

By bike

The easy way. Free guarded parking for 400 bikes in the old loading yard, and the riverside cycle path passes the gate.

Parking

Limited on-site spaces, €8 per evening, reserve with your ticket. Better: Park+Ride Wondelgem, then tram. Ghent's low-emission zone applies to the centre.

04 — Accessibility

Room for everyone

Step-free
Gate, courtyard, Mash Hall and toilets are level. The Cellar is reached by a wide lift (1.4 m).
Seating
Wheelchair spaces and companion seats in both rooms. Book on access@lanternnights.example; companions enter free.
Hearing
Induction loop in Mash Hall. Quiet seats near the stage on request.
Quiet space
A low-light, low-sound room in the old malt store, open all day.
Assistance
Assistance dogs welcome. Staff in orange shirts can guide you to your seat.
05 — Good to know

Quick answers

What should I wear?

Whatever you walked or cycled in. Sneakers, jeans and jumpers are the house style. It gets cool in the Cellar after dark, so bring a layer.

Can I bring drinks and food into the concerts?

Yes, drinks from our bars are welcome in both rooms, in reusable cups. Outside food and glass bottles stay in the courtyard.

Are seats reserved?

No. Doors open 30 minutes before each concert and seating is first come, first served. Pass holders can queue early for the headline L-tier concerts.

What if it rains, or I can't come?

All concerts are indoors. Passes can be transferred to a friend until 1 August 2027, and refunded in full up to 30 days before the festival.
