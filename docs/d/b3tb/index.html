Read the waiver

The short version. Bouldering has no ropes, so falling is part of it.

  1. Climbing and falling can hurt you. You climb at your own risk.
  2. Land on the mats feet first, and never climb above or below someone else.
  3. Watch the 10-minute safety video at the desk if it's your first visit.
  4. Tell staff about injuries straight away. There's a first-aider on every shift.
Read the full waiver

By entering Overhang Climbing Ltd's premises you accept that climbing is an activity with a risk of personal injury or death. You agree to follow the posted wall rules and staff instructions, to check the matting is clear before you climb and descend, and to keep children under 14 within arm's reach. Overhang is not liable for injury except where caused by our negligence. Your details are stored for 3 years after your last visit, then deleted.
