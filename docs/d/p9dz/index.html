04 · The farm

Concrete, then sixty centimetres of soil.

Until 2016 this quay moved fruit from ships to lorries. In 2019 we laid down a root barrier, trucked in clean clay from a polder in the Hoeksche Waard, and started planting. Three growers, a lot of volunteers, and a barge for a shed.

05 · Questions

Fair questions

Isn’t harbour ground polluted?

The old quay is sealed under a root barrier. Everything grows in 60 cm of clean clay and our own compost laid on top. An independent lab tests the soil and a sample of crops every spring; the 2026 report is pinned on the barge and we email it to members.

Can I choose what goes in my box?

Not item by item. The box follows the season, which is the whole idea. You can set up to three permanent swaps (say, no celeriac, ever) and we replace them with something else from that week’s harvest.

What if I’m away, or forget to pick up?

Pause any week in your account or by replying to the Sunday email, until Monday 20:00. Paused weeks are free. Boxes not picked up by closing go to the Voedselbank Rotterdam the next morning, so nothing is wasted.

How do I pay, and how do I stop?

We bill by iDEAL or direct debit at the start of each month, only for the boxes you’ll receive. There’s no contract and no minimum. Cancel in your account and your last box is the next one we pack.
