LanternNights.

Three evenings of chamber music in a brewery that stopped making beer in 1998. Sit on a crate, hear a string quartet next to the copper kettles. Sneakers are fine.

The brewery

Brouwerij Van Hoecke has stood on the Dampoort canal since 1887. The brewing hall is our main stage; the old fermentation cellar is the small one.

Good questions

What should I wear?

Whatever you walked in with. The hall is cool even in August (about 19°C), so bring a layer. Cobbles in the courtyard are hard on heels.

Can I bring my own food and drink?

No outside alcohol, but water bottles are welcome. The courtyard kitchen serves vegetarian and vegan plates from €9, and the bar pours three Ghent beers plus natural wine.

Do I need to book a seat with a day pass?

No. Seating in both rooms is first come, first served, with doors 30 minutes early. Only the Festival pass includes a reserved seat in Mash Hall.

What happens if it rains?

Nothing changes. All concerts are indoors; only the courtyard bar is open-air and it has a covered side. Concerts are never cancelled for weather.
