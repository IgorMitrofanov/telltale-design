A farm on ground that was never meant for one

Kade 7 was a container quay until 2019. Under the paving lies harbour fill, so we do not dig. Every bed sits on 40 cm of our own compost, above a root barrier. The vegetables never touch the old ground.

Questions

How do I pause a week?

Open your account and press Pause on the week you want to skip. Do it before Monday 12:00. We do not charge that week and we do not harvest for you.

What if I cannot collect on Thursday?

Your box waits in our cold room at the pickup point until Friday 12:00. After that it goes to the food bank in Delfshaven and we still charge the week. If you know in advance, pause instead.

Can I choose what goes in the box?

No, the box holds what is ready that week. You can list up to 3 vegetables you do not eat. We replace them with something else of the same weight.

Where does the cargo bike go?

To any address within 6 km of Kade 7, on Thursday between 16:00 and 19:00. Enter your postcode when you sign up and we tell you at once if it is outside the route.
