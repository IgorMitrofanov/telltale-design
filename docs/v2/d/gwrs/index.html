04

The waiver bit

Short version: climbing is brilliant and occasionally ouchy. Please read it properly anyway.

Overhang participation agreement

  1. Bouldering involves falling. Even onto deep matting, falls can cause sprains, breaks and worse. You accept that risk when you climb here.
  2. You'll watch the induction video or get a walkthrough from staff before your first climb, and follow the wall rules posted by the desk.
  3. Don't climb above or below someone else, keep the mats clear, and down-climb where you can rather than jumping from the top.
  4. Holds can spin or break. If one moves, come off safely and tell the desk — we'll fix it, and buy you a brew for the trouble.
  5. Under-18s need a named parent or guardian who agrees to these terms on their behalf. Under-14s must be supervised by that adult at all times.
  6. Overhang Climbing Ltd is not liable for injury except where caused by our negligence. Nothing here limits your statutory rights.
