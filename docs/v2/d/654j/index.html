Getting there

The brewery sits on the old Sluizeken dock, 2 km from the centre of Ghent. Walk the last two minutes along the water.

By train

Get off at Gent-Sint-Pieters. Trains run from Brussels in 35 minutes and from Antwerp in 50. Outside the station, take bus 3 to Brouwerijstraat (12 minutes, every 10 minutes until 01:00 during the festival). The brewery gate is 2 minutes on foot.

By bike

From the station, follow the canal path north for 4 km (15 minutes). We run a staffed bike park for 400 bikes at the Kaaistraat gate, free for ticket holders. Blue-bike shared bikes are available at the station.

By car

The brewery has no public parking. Leave your car at P+R Gentbrugge (€4 a day) and take bus 3 for 18 minutes. Two accessible bays are held at the gate: reserve one at least 3 days ahead through tickets@lanternnights.example.

Free companion ticket

If you need support to attend, your companion enters free. Write to us before you book.

Quiet room

The Hop Room is open all day on the ground floor, away from the music, with soft light and seats.

Assistance dogs and print

Assistance dogs are welcome in both halls. Large-print programmes are at the foyer desk.

Questions

Something else? Write to hello@lanternnights.example. We answer within two working days.

Is there a dress code?

No. Sneakers, work boots and linen are all fine. The brewery floor is concrete and cools down in the evening, so bring a jumper.

Can I eat and drink during the festival?

Yes. The brewery bar pours beer from the tanks next door and serves hot food between concerts. Drinks stay in the foyer and courtyard. You can bring water into both halls.

Can I change or refund my ticket?

You can move a pass to another name for free until 7 August 2027. You can get a refund until 15 July 2027. We keep the €2 service fee.

How do Under-26 tickets work?

You pay €20 at the door and show an ID with your date of birth. Doors open one hour before the first concert each day. The halls can sell out on Saturday, so come early for the evening concerts.
