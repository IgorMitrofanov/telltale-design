Getting to the brewery

The old Van Hoecke brewery stands on the canal, six minutes on foot from Gent-Dampoort station.

Former Van Hoecke breweryBrouwersgang 12, 9000 Ghent

By train
Take any train to Gent-Dampoort, then walk 6 minutes along the canal. From Gent-Sint-Pieters it is a 12-minute ride.
The last trains leave Dampoort at 23:41 on Saturday and Sunday, 22:52 on Monday.
By bike
The bike park at the gate has 300 racks and a staffed corner. It is free and open from 10:00 until the last concert ends.
Cargo bikes and trailers go in the courtyard lane.
By car
The brewery has no parking. Use the Dampoort car park, 5 minutes on foot. It costs €2.50 per hour, capped at €15 per day.
Blue-badge holders can drive to the loading dock. Tell us your plate in advance.

Access

Both stages are reachable without steps. Tell us what you need and we will set it up before you arrive.

  • Step-free entranceA ramp at the main gate and a level path across the courtyard.
  • Lift to the Cellar1.8 m deep, fits a wheelchair and a companion. Staff run it during all concerts.
  • Wheelchair places8 in Kettle Hall and 4 in the Cellar, each with a seat for a companion.
  • HearingAn induction loop covers the front 12 rows of Kettle Hall. Receivers at the desk are free.
  • Quiet roomA room in the old malt store, open all day, with a live feed of both stages.
  • ToiletsTwo step-free toilets in the courtyard, one with a changing table.
  • Assistance dogsWelcome in all rooms. A water bowl is at the bar.
  • Large-print programmeFree at the desk, or sent by post if you write to us before 1 August.

Questions about access? Write to access@lanternnights.example or call +32 9 555 01 27 on weekdays from 10:00 to 16:00.

Questions

Can I come in sneakers and a hoodie?

Yes. The floor is old brewery concrete and the Cellar stays at 14 °C all year, so a jumper is a good idea. Nobody checks your clothes.

What if I arrive after a concert has started?

Staff seat latecomers at the first break between pieces, so you will not walk in during a quiet passage. If you miss a whole concert on a Day pass, you can still enter the next one.

Can I bring my child?

Yes. Children under 12 enter free with an adult. The Sunday 13:00 concert is written for ages 5 and up. For the others, we suggest ages 10 and up, because the pieces run 60 to 80 minutes.

Can I change or refund my ticket?

You can move a Day pass to another day until 12:00 the day before, at no cost. We refund tickets up to 14 days before the festival, minus a €3 fee. If a concert is cancelled, we refund its single ticket in full.
