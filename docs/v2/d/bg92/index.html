How the box works

Three decisions, made once. After that the box arrives every week until you pause it or stop. There is no contract and no sign-up fee.

  1. 1

    Choose a size

    Small feeds one or two people for a week of dinners. Large is for three to five. You can change size for any week.

  2. 2

    Collect it or have it cycled over

    Pick up your box on Thursday at one of four points in the city, or we bring it to your door by cargo bike for €4 extra.

  3. 3

    Pause any week

    Away or still have leeks? Pause before Sunday 20:00 and you pay nothing that week. Reply “pause” to the Monday email, or use your account.

A farm on a quay

Until 1995, ships unloaded fruit on the Keilehaven quay. In 2021 the port let us use a 2-hectare strip of it that nobody needed.

We laid clean soil on top of the concrete and planted 54 beds, three polytunnels and a compost yard. We grow without synthetic pesticides or fertiliser. Everything in your box comes from this site, and most of it was in the ground three days before you eat it.

The beds never touch the old harbour ground. The drawing shows what is under them.

Questions people ask first

Anything else? Email hello@polderenplot.nl. Anouk answers within one working day.

Is it safe to grow food on an old harbour site?

Yes. The beds do not touch the original ground. They sit on 60 cm of clean sand and soil over the concrete quay deck, with a root barrier between them.

An independent lab tests the soil and a sample of crops every spring for heavy metals and oil residues. You can read the 2026 report in the farm shop or ask us for the PDF.

How do I pause or stop?

To pause a week, reply “pause” to the Monday email or use your account before Sunday 20:00. A paused week costs nothing. You can pause as many weeks as you like.

To stop, tell us before Sunday 20:00. Your last box comes that Thursday. There is no notice period and no fee.

What if I miss my pickup?

Boxes that are not collected on Thursday go back to the cold store at the farm. Collect yours on Saturday between 10:00 and 13:00.

If nobody collects a box by Saturday 13:00, we give the vegetables to a food bank in Delfshaven. We still charge for that week.

Can I leave out vegetables I don’t eat?

Yes. Name up to three vegetables you never want, for example beetroot or celeriac. We replace them with something else from that week’s harvest of about the same weight.

If you have an allergy, tell us when you sign up. We pack all boxes in one shed, so we cannot promise that no trace of another vegetable is left.
